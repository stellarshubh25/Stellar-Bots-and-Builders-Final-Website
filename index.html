We build simple websites for small businesses: services, timings, address, map, and call and WhatsApp buttons, in your own words and photos.

HOW IT WORKS

A small process with no mystery.

You see everything before it goes live.

  1. Send us your details Services, timings, address, phone number and photos. Prices too, if you want them shown.
  2. We build and test it We design your site and set up your assistant, then check both on a phone, including questions the assistant should not answer.
  3. You review, then it goes live You ask for changes. Once you are happy, we set up hosting and give you your link, domain or QR code.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Clear about what it can and cannot do.

Everything is built from the information you give us, and you approve the text before launch. The assistant answers common questions. It does not book appointments, take payments or replace you.

We do not promise rankings or sales. A good site and a quick answer make you easier to find and contact, but results depend on your business and your location.
