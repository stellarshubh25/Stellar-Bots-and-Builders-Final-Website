HOW IT WORKS

A small process with no mystery.

You see how the assistant answers before any customer does.

  1. Send us your details Services, prices, timings, address and phone number. A message or a photo of your price list is enough to start.
  2. We set it up and test it We load your information and try it with real customer-style questions, including ones it should not answer.
  3. You check it, then it goes live You try it on your phone first. When you are happy, you get a link and a QR code to share with customers.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Clear about what it can and cannot do.

It answers common questions about your business. It does not book appointments, take payments or replace you.

For anything outside your information, it points the customer to your phone number, so you stay in charge of the conversation.
