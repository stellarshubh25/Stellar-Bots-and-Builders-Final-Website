How it works

Customerasks a question
Your assistantanswers from your information
Youcustomer contacts you when the assistant is not sure
  1. You send us your details: services, prices, timings, booking and payment rules, and the questions customers often ask.
  2. We set up your assistant with that information and test it with realistic questions.
  3. You get a link to share, or a code to place on your website.
  4. We update your information when things change, and use the saved questions to fix weak answers.

Good to know

  • The assistant answers only from the information you give us. It does not guess prices or timings.
  • It does not take payments or confirm bookings. It tells customers how to book with you.
  • For clinics, it does not give medical advice. It points patients to the clinic.
  • It saves you time on repeated questions. We do not promise more sales.
