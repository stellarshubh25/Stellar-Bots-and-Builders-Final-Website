WHAT WE DO

Built around the questions people actually ask.

We map the repeated conversations in your inbox, then turn them into a simple, natural flow your customers can use on their phone.

That can mean a first-response bot, a product helper, a lead qualification flow, or a small support centre.

01

Customer support chat

Answer common questions, share the right link, and hand off the complicated stuff to a person.

02

Lead and enquiry flows

Ask sensible questions upfront so your team gets the context they need before they reply.

03

Website conversations

Give visitors a useful route through your site instead of making them search through pages.

HOW IT WORKS

A small process with no mystery.

We keep the work visible. You see the flow, the words, and the interface before anything goes live.

  1. Start with your inbox We look at real questions, recurring problems, and the words your customers already use.
  2. Write the conversation We shape short prompts and useful replies in your voice. English, Hindi, and Hinglish can all be planned where relevant.
  3. Test it on a phone We check the paths, tap targets, and handoff points on the screen your customers are most likely to use.
